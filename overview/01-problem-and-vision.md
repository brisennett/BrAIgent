# Problem and Vision

## The Problem

Support today is reactive. A customer hits a wall, submits a ticket, and waits. Someone on the team manually reads it, figures out what information is missing, asks follow-up questions, researches the issue, and writes a response — often starting from scratch even when we've seen the same problem before.

This works at a certain scale. It stops working when the customer base grows faster than the team.

Four specific gaps:

**No self-service path.** Customers who could solve their own problem have nowhere to go. The only option is a ticket.

**Unstructured intake.** "Describe your issue" produces paragraphs of context-free text. Reps spend the first exchange just figuring out what the customer is actually asking.

**Inconsistent research.** Whether a rep finds the right answer depends on how long they've been on the team and whether they happen to remember a similar case. That knowledge doesn't transfer.

**Every response is written from scratch.** Even for issues we've resolved ten times before.

---

## The Vision

An AI layer that sits between the customer and the support team. It doesn't replace the team — it does the time-consuming parts that shouldn't require a human.

Before a ticket is created, it tries to resolve the issue using existing documentation and playbooks. If it can't, it collects the right information from the customer so the rep doesn't have to. Once a case exists, it researches across every data source we have and puts a draft response in front of the rep for review.

The rep decides what gets sent. Always.

---

## What Success Looks Like

- Fewer tickets created because customers found the answer before submitting
- Cases that do get created arrive with complete information attached
- Reps spend time on judgment calls, not research and drafting
- Resolution time drops without adding headcount
